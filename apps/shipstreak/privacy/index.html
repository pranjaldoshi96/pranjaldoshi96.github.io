Privacy Policy

ShipStreak · Last updated July 23, 2026

ShipStreak does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Privacy question? Email us

Overview

ShipStreak is an offline-first iPhone app for recording one concrete thing you ship each day. It works without an account, backend, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

When you create a shipment entry, the app stores the title, category, optional URL, learning note, and completion date in ShipStreak’s local app container on your device.

ShipStreak’s developer does not receive or have access to this information. Your device and backup settings are managed by you through Apple’s system settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports sent to us
  • No payment or financial information
  • No browsing history, contacts, photos, or other device data

Third parties and external links

ShipStreak contains no third-party software development kits and sends no app data to third parties. If you open a URL saved in an entry, your browser opens that external website and its privacy policy applies.

Retention and deletion

Entries remain on your device until you delete them from the Archive or remove the app. Because we do not receive your data, there is no server-side account or copy for us to delete.

Children’s privacy

ShipStreak does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If ShipStreak’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant app version is released. The date at the top shows when the policy was last revised.
