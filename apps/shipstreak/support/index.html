Common questions

The useful answers first.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are stored inside ShipStreak’s local app container on your iPhone. ShipStreak does not run a server and cannot see your entries.

Do I need an account?

No. ShipStreak has no sign-up, login, subscription, or account system.

How do I delete an entry?

Open Archive, swipe left on an entry, and choose Delete. You can also open the entry and use its delete action. ShipStreak asks for confirmation first.

Why did my current streak stay active?

If you shipped yesterday but have not logged today yet, your current streak remains active for the day. It resets only after a full day is missed.

Which iPhones are supported?

ShipStreak supports iPhones running iOS 17 or later.

Privacy questions

ShipStreak collects no personal data and stores your shipment log on your device.
