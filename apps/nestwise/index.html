01 · Know

What's normal this week.

Every day shows what to expect at your baby's age, what to do, and the warning signs. Plus step-by-step guides for fever, jaundice, crying, poop colours, latching and safe sleep.

02 · Log

One tap, one hand.

Breastfeed and sleep timers, bottles and diapers in a single tap. See feeds, sleep and wet diapers for the last 24 hours, and share a summary with your doctor.

03 · Local

Your country, your schedule.

India and US vaccine schedules with reminders, local emergency numbers and helplines, and the family advice you'll hear — kajal, gripe water, tummy sleeping — checked against current guidance.

Private by design

Your baby's data stays on your phone.

Nestwise has no accounts, servers, advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. Nothing you log ever leaves your iPhone.

Not medical advice

Nestwise gives general information for parents. It doesn't diagnose and isn't a substitute for your doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
