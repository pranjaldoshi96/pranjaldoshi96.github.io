Common questions

The useful answers first.

How do I control the hopper?

The hopper jumps automatically. Hold the left half of the screen to steer left or the right half to steer right. Release to reduce horizontal acceleration.

What counts as a perfect landing?

Land near the glowing center of the pad. Perfect landings score three points and increase your combo. Edge landings keep the run alive but reset the combo.

Why did the next pad move?

Rough landings create stronger ripples that temporarily disturb upcoming pads. Moving pads are violet, and one-use fragile pads are coral.

Does Daily Challenge need internet?

No. Ripple Hop generates the day's challenge offline from the UTC date. Your attempts and best score stay on your iPhone.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Open Settings from the Home screen and switch Sound or Haptics off. Ripple Hop also respects the silent switch and mixes with audio from other apps.

Which iPhones are supported?

Ripple Hop supports iPhones running iOS 17 or later.

Privacy questions

Ripple Hop collects no personal data and stores settings and scores on your device.
