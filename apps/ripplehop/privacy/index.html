Privacy Policy

Ripple Hop · Last updated August 2, 2026

Ripple Hop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Privacy question? Email us

Overview

Ripple Hop is an offline portrait iPhone game. It works without an account, backend, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network access.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings, tutorial completion, high scores, Daily Challenge attempts, and gameplay statistics in Ripple Hop's local app container on your device.

Ripple Hop's developer does not receive or have access to this information. Your device and backup settings are managed by you through Apple's system settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, account, or contact information
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No device or advertising identifiers
  • No gameplay analytics or diagnostics sent to the developer
  • No payment or financial information
  • No contacts, photos, browsing history, or other device data

Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge uses the UTC date on your device to generate the same deterministic pad sequence for that day. The date and your best score remain on your device and are not transmitted.

Third parties

Ripple Hop contains no third-party software development kits and sends no app data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Settings and statistics remain on your device until you remove the app or erase its data through iOS. Because we do not receive your data, there is no server-side account or copy for us to delete.

Children's privacy

Ripple Hop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Ripple Hop's data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant app version is released. The date at the top shows when the policy was last revised.
