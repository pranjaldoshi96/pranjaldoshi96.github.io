Privacy Policy

Techgist · Last updated November 3, 2026

Techgist does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. What you read and save stays on your iPhone.

Privacy question? Email us

Overview

Techgist is an iPhone news reader for technology, AI, startup and developer news. It works without an account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the stories you save, the sources you turn off, your daily digest and summary settings, and a cache of recent stories and images so the app opens quickly and works offline. All of it lives in Techgist' local app container on your device.

Techgist' developer does not receive or have access to this information.

Where stories come from

To show stories, the app downloads a list of the day's stories from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), where Techgist publishes it. If that list is out of date, the app downloads public news feeds and article pages directly from the publishers, such as techcrunch.com and theverge.com. Article images load from the publishers' servers.

These are ordinary web requests. Like any website, GitHub and the publishers receive standard request information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The requests contain nothing about you, what you read, or what you saved.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, account, or contact information
  • No reading history, saved stories, or preferences sent anywhere
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No device or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or diagnostics sent to the developer
  • No payment or financial information

On-device summaries

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, story summaries are written on your device by Apple's on-device model. Article text is not sent to the developer or to any AI service.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily digest, iOS asks for permission to show notifications. The digest is scheduled locally on your device and contains a headline. Nothing is sent to a server.

If you turn on big story alerts, the app checks for major stories when iOS refreshes it in the background and shows the alert itself. Alerts are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Reading full articles

When you open a story, it loads in Safari's in-app browser directly from the publisher. That publisher's own privacy policy applies to its website.

Third parties

Techgist contains no third-party software development kits and sends no app data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Your saved stories and settings remain on your device until you remove the app. Because we do not receive your data, there is no server-side account or copy for us to delete.

Children's privacy

Techgist does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Techgist' data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant app version is released. The date at the top shows when the policy was last revised.
