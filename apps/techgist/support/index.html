Common questions

The useful answers first.

How often do stories update?

New stories arrive throughout the day. The app checks when you open it, and you can pull down on any tab to check right away.

Why do some cards say "+2 more"?

Several outlets covered the same story. Techgist merges them into one card and ranks stories with wider coverage higher.

Why don't I see AI summaries?

Summaries need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, and the publisher's feed must include enough of the article. Otherwise you see the publisher's own excerpt. You can turn summaries off in Settings.

How do I hide a source?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and switch off any source. Its stories disappear from every tab.

How do I get the daily digest?

Settings → Daily digest. Pick a time, and you'll get one notification a day with the top story.

How do big story alerts work?

Settings → Big story alerts. You get a notification when a story is covered by several outlets at once, at most 3 a day. Alerts are delivered through iCloud, so you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Where are my saved stories?

Tap the bookmark icon at the top right. Swipe left on a story to remove it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The latest stories you loaded stay available, and saved stories are kept on your iPhone. Opening a full article needs a connection.

Which iPhones are supported?

Techgist supports iPhones running iOS 17 or later. On-device summaries need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence on iOS 26 or later.

Privacy questions

Techgist collects no personal data. What you read and save stays on your device.
