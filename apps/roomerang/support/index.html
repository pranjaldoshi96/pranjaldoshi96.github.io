Common questions

The useful answers first.

I am stuck on a trial. Is it broken?

Open the hint panel with the question mark. Every trial gives you two nudges before the outright answer, and all of them are free and unlimited. If a trial still will not budge, it is worth emailing us the number.

Can I skip a trial?

Yes. A Skip this one button sits under the room on every trial, it costs nothing, and it never runs out. A skipped trial is recorded as skipped rather than solved, so your map stays honest.

The door looks open but will not let me through.

The door fills as you get closer to satisfying the trial, so a partly filled door means partly done. It only opens when the whole requirement is met.

A trial wants me to do something with the phone itself.

Some trials are solved by tilting, shaking, charging, muting, or laying down the iPhone. If you would rather not move the phone, turn on Assistive controls in Settings and an on-screen button appears in its place.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the map screen and switch Sound or Vibration off. Roomerang also follows the silent switch and mixes with audio from other apps.

Can I start over?

Settings, then Erase all progress. All thirty trials go back to unsolved. It cannot be undone.

Which iPhones are supported?

Roomerang supports iPhones running iOS 17 or later.
