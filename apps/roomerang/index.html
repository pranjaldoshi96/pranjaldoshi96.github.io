Thirty single ideas

Obvious afterwards. Opaque until then.

01 · Look

The room never changes shape.

What changes is what you have to realise. A wall that lies about its own number. A door that will not take a single finger seriously.

02 · Reach

The phone is part of the puzzle.

Some trials are solved by tilting, shaking, charging, muting, or laying the iPhone face down. Roomerang never changes a setting for you. It just notices.

03 · Unlearn

Habits become traps.

A third of the trials subvert something the earlier ones deliberately taught you. Every door fills as you get closer, so you always know whether what you just did counted.

✓

Private by design

A game, not a data pipeline.

Roomerang has no accounts, network requests, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party SDKs. Every hint, answer, and skip is free.
