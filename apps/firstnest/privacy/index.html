Privacy Policy

Firstnest · Last updated November 3, 2026

Firstnest does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Privacy question? Email us

Overview

Firstnest is an iPhone guide and tracker for new parents. It works without an account, backend, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network access.

Information stored on your device

The app stores what you enter: your baby's name or nickname, birthday and country; feeding, sleep, diaper and growth logs; vaccine records; your daily mood check-ins; and your unit and reminder preferences. All of it lives in Firstnest's local app container on your device.

Firstnest's developer does not receive or have access to this information. Your device and backup settings are managed by you through Apple's system settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, account, or contact information
  • No health, feeding, sleep, vaccine or mood data sent anywhere
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No device or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or diagnostics sent to the developer
  • No payment or financial information
  • No contacts, photos, browsing history, or other device data

Notifications

If you turn on vaccine reminders, iOS asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and contain only the visit name and your baby's name or nickname. Nothing is sent to a server.

Sharing a summary

The "Share a summary for the doctor" button opens the standard iOS share sheet with a text summary. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Third parties

Firstnest contains no third-party software development kits and sends no app data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove the app, or until you use Delete all data in the app's Settings. Because we do not receive your data, there is no server-side account or copy for us to delete.

Children's privacy

Firstnest is designed for adult parents and caregivers. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Firstnest provides general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes to this policy

If Firstnest's data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant app version is released. The date at the top shows when the policy was last revised.
