Common questions

The useful answers first.

Which countries are supported?

India and the United States. Your country sets the vaccine schedule, emergency numbers, helplines and local advice. Change it any time in You → Settings → Country.

The vaccine dates don't match my doctor's.

Firstnest shows the standard national schedule from your baby's birthday. Doctors often adjust timings, and government and private schedules differ slightly. Always follow your doctor — you can record the actual date by tapping a vaccine.

How do I log a feed or sleep that already happened?

Go to the Log tab, tap +, choose the type, then pick "Already happened" and set the time and length.

I forgot to stop a timer.

Stop it, then swipe left on the entry in the Log tab to delete it and add it again with the right times.

Can my partner see the same log?

Not yet — data stays on one iPhone. Partner sync is planned for a future version.

How do I switch between kg/ml and lb/oz?

You → Settings → Units.

How do I delete everything?

You → Settings → Delete all data. It removes every log, vaccine record and check-in from your iPhone and can't be undone.

Which iPhones are supported?

Firstnest supports iPhones running iOS 17 or later.
